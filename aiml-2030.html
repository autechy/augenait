The engineering ladder

Foundations still matter

The keynote frames AI engineering as a layered journey: AI → Machine Learning → Deep Learning → Transformers → Generative AI → Copilots → Agents → Agentic Systems → 2030 AI Engineering.

01 · Foundations

Become a Programmer

Python, DSA, Git, SQL, and math foundations are the Year 1 base.

02 · Build

Become an ML Builder

Year 2 focuses on NumPy/Pandas, classical ML, evaluation, and real datasets.

03 · Specialize

Become an AI Builder

Year 3 moves into deep learning, LLMs, RAG, multimodal models, and agentic AI.

04 · Production

Become an AI Engineer

Year 4 adds system design, deployment, MLOps/LLMOps, safety, monitoring, and production skills.

05 · Proof of Work

Portfolio over certificates

The keynote emphasizes projects that are built, deployed, documented, and demo-able.

06 · Human skills

Communicate and evaluate

Communication, product thinking, problem solving, critical evaluation, and adaptability remain core skills.

Year 1 target

Programmer first

Programming

Python syntax, OOP, loops, functions, recursion, collections, files, exceptions, debugging, virtual environments.

Developer fundamentals

Git & GitHub, Linux basics, CLI, HTTP, JSON, REST APIs, VS Code.

DSA + SQL

Arrays, strings, hashing, trees, graphs, stacks/queues, sorting/searching, SELECT/JOIN/GROUP BY.

Math

Algebra, probability, statistics, vectors, matrices, and basic calculus.

Build evidence

Project mindset

The keynote's direction is simple: build small projects early, then move toward applied ML, GenAI/agent projects, and production-grade systems.

8+Year 1 build projects
10Year 2 ML projects
10Year 3 GenAI/agent projects
5Year 4 production projects
Responsible AI

Amplify your brain — don't replace it

The keynote recommends using AI as a tutor, debugger, reviewer, simulator, research assistant, brainstormer, interviewer, and practice generator — while avoiding blind copying, unverified answers, fabricated references, and submitting AI work as your own.

Learning loop

Attempt → AI → Critique → Verify → Improve

Production thinking

Understand → Build → Evaluate → Deploy → Think → Communicate
