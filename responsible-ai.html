Use AI well

Responsible AI

The course outcome explicitly includes ethical, responsible, and secure use of Generative AI tools.

Hallucinations

AI can produce plausible but incorrect information. Verify facts and important claims.

Academic integrity

Use AI within your institution's rules and make sure you understand your submission.

Privacy

Do not paste passwords, private records, or confidential data into AI tools.

Copyright

Review how generated or imported content may be used before publishing or submitting it.

Bias

AI outputs can reflect bias or incomplete context. Review and challenge outputs.

Verification

For high-stakes or technical claims, use trusted sources and cross-check.

Helpful prompt

Learn with AI

“Explain recursion using a simple example suitable for a first-year engineering student.”

Poor academic use

Outsource the assignment

“Complete my entire assignment so I can submit it as my own.”
