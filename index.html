Course outcomes

Learn to use AI with purpose

The syllabus focuses on tool selection, prompting, tool comparison, digital content creation, and ethical, responsible, secure use of Generative AI.

Outcome 01

Choose the right tool

Identify real-world problems and select an appropriate Generative AI tool.

Outcome 02

Prompt effectively

Apply prompt engineering techniques to generate accurate and context-aware outputs.

Outcome 03

Compare tools

Identify and compare different Generative AI tools for different needs.

Outcome 04

Create digital content

Create text, images, presentations, and other digital content.

Outcome 05

Use AI responsibly

Employ Generative AI tools ethically, responsibly, and securely.

Student principle

Use AI to strengthen your understanding, not replace it.
