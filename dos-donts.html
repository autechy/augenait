Responsible habits

Do's & Don'ts

Simple rules for getting value from AI without losing your own understanding or compromising privacy and academic integrity.

DO

Use AI intentionally

Verify important information.
Understand generated code before using it.
Use AI as a learning assistant.
Protect personal and confidential data.
Review and improve AI output.
Cite sources where your course or assignment requires it.
DON'T

Don't outsource your learning

Don't blindly copy AI output.
Don't submit work you do not understand.
Don't share passwords, secrets, or sensitive information.
Don't assume every AI answer is correct.
Don't use AI to bypass academic requirements.
Don't become completely dependent on a single tool.
